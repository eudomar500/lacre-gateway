WHY GENLAYER

01

Keys obtained
by consensus

Validators each fetch the sender's signing key and must agree on it, so no single resolver decides.

02

New senders
as data

An unknown domain needs no integration — its key is fetched and recorded the first time it signs.

03

Interpretation
by consensus

What the email says is accepted only when independent validators read it the same way.
